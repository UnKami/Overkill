# Overkill 0.17.0 — Readable Combat and Cinematic Clarity

On GitHub: **UnKami/Overkill → Releases → 0.17.0 → Assets → OverkillSetup-0.17.0.exe**. The installer and portable ZIP contain the same game. Play Sentinel opens the rigged encounter in a separate profile.

## Player-facing changes

- Larger horizontal choices stay at the top with no shared panel background or bottom dock. Essential relic text is 24 design pixels (16 pixels at 720p), with a large-text option. Explicit bind buttons are 48 design pixels tall; artwork remains safe to inspect without committing.
- Replacement options and Keep & Sweep are grouped together. The destination, discarded relic and sweep order are visible before commitment. Inspect Battlefield is visible again, with its I shortcut.
- Hover/focus previews calculate resulting HP and Block from current state, including persistent block, multi-hit, lifesteal, strength, weakness, vulnerability, thorns, bleed, hazards and the next-attack multiplier. Forecasts stop at concealed actions or an enemy defeat; they do not reveal future enemies/actions or promise their outcomes. They forecast HP/Block, not currency.
- The enemy clock spells out revealed actions. Attack values are labeled base values; the outcome preview accounts for modifiers. Hour numbers sit outside the relic artwork; reduced ornament makes values easier to read.
- A Combat Log preserves the latest 16 damage, block and healing events. HP is no longer duplicated in the top bar. Overkill is named, the obsolete turn-reset Block tooltip is corrected, and keyword explanations are available in help and relic tooltips.
- Map destinations have readable names, current/visited markers, route emphasis and hover/focus encounter details. Shops show prices and name Overkill instead of an unexplained abbreviation. Larger shared relic descriptions improve shops, rewards and collection screens.

## Art direction and timing

- Reframed the rigged arena below the decision area, with less glossy worn metal and more restrained cool/warm lighting. The clock faces have less competing ornament.
- Illustrated fighters are larger and grounded with contact shadows. Illustrated attacks now have a deliberate windup and contact signal. Their damage and impact effects wait for the contact pose, matching the cinematic encounter's timing model.
- The 3D viewport is capped at 1600 pixels and follows smaller window widths, with 2x MSAA. This reduces rendering cost without shrinking the UI; it is not a 60 fps guarantee.

## Scope and compatibility

The nine-hour clock, twelve starting relics and gradual enemy reveals remain unchanged. Existing 0.14–0.16 saves remain compatible. Source: `v0.17.0-test`, branch `feat/yonatan-readable-cinematic`; gameplay awaits review before merging into main.

This is a development playtest, not finished AAA production. Bespoke character art, animation variety, broad hardware performance and human boss-balance testing remain open. Illustrated and rigged encounters still use different art pipelines. The unsigned installer is compiled; its interactive wizard is not covered by gameplay tests. Existing certificate-store and headless shutdown warnings remain.

## Verification

The release is gated on source and exported gameplay/preview checks, all-relic typography checks, rendered assembly/replacement/inspection layouts at 1080p and 720p large text, ultrawide bounds, and contact timing in normal/fast/reduced-motion modes. Published assets are checked against local SHA-256 hashes and their download URLs before delivery.
