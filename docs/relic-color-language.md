# The Five Essences of the Chronometer

Relics are not painted by rarity or decoration. Their emitted color is a readable statement of what the mechanism does when its hour resolves.

| Essence | Color | Mechanical promise | Lore |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Ember | Orange `#FF8A1F` | Deals attack damage | Heat trapped at the instant a weapon strikes. |
| Aegis | Blue `#4FB8FF` | Grants persistent Block | Crystallized time that refuses an incoming blow. |
| Dominion | Purple `#B76CFF` | Strengthens, heals, retaliates, or empowers a later action | Will imposed on the mechanism itself. |
| Blight | Green `#58D66B` | Applies Bleed, Weak, Vulnerable, or another enemy debuff | Corrupted time carried into the opposing clock. |
| Blood | Blood red `#C41734` | Directly grants Overkill currency | Lethality condensed into value before a death occurs. |

Blackened metal, glass, and neutral highlights may frame any relic, but the magical emission follows these rules. Blood red is reserved for direct Overkill generation; ordinary attacks never use it. A two-color relic must perform both advertised effects. Three-color relics are intentionally excluded from the standard lineup so every object remains readable at clock-socket size.

## Presentation rules

- Relic art is a single centered object on transparency, never a scenery card.
- The primary essence colors the card border, clock socket, activation relay, and default attack accent.
- A secondary essence appears as a second edge marker and must be visible in the object itself.
- The reliquary legend teaches the mapping in-world; tooltips spell out the binding for accessibility.
- Upgrades increase numbers but never change a relic's binding or color identity.
