# Overkill cinematic gameplay trailer — 2026-09-30

## Creative direction

**Emotional hook:** *When death isn't enough.* The excess wound becomes an asset, then a difficult purchase. The trailer begins with the solitary Executioner and ends with a final guardian, tying the player's power to what each defeat leaves behind.

**Format:** 44.33 seconds, 1920 × 1080, 30 fps, H.264 MP4 with stereo AAC. The source is a deterministic Godot 4.5.1 capture of game scenes, edited with the game's `clockwork_nocturne.wav` score and combat cues. The capture stages specific combat and currency values for a readable showcase; it is a promotional sequence, not footage of a continuous human playthrough.

## New boss reward design

Act bosses now route to **The Overkill Altar** instead of the ordinary free relic reward. The normal reward remains for other kills. The altar shows three exclusive Zenith relics with true combat effects: The Last Bell (14 Block, 3 Thorns, 1 Strength; 25 OK), The Debt Crown (2 Vulnerable, two 10-damage lifesteal hits; 35 OK), and Zenith Prism (7 damage, 2 Strength, next attack ×3; 45 OK). The player may buy one, or leave and retain the bank. A full 12-copy chronometer requires a specific replacement choice before the charge. The next-act and final-boss transitions remain in the established campaign flow.

The existing Clockwright shop already spends Overkill Points earlier in a run. The altar is the first **boss-exclusive Zenith purchase opportunity**, not the first possible currency spend in the game. The generated background and transparent object art follow `docs/visual_canon.md`; UI labels and prices are native controls above the art. Zenith relics are excluded from generic shops and rewards.

## Reproducibility and verification

- Source capture: `trailer/capture.tscn` / `trailer/capture.gd` in an isolated Godot user profile. It records a lossless-editable MJPEG AVI in `.tools/trailer-video/` and selected native PNG frames in `trailer/captures/`.
- Edit: `trailer/render.py` uses FFmpeg to add three restrained title treatments, the existing score and four existing combat cues; output is `trailer/Overkill_Cinematic_Gameplay_Trailer_2026-09-30.mp4`.
- Native altar test: `trailer/altar_test.tscn` verifies all three offers, Zenith exclusivity, 12-copy replacement, exact 35-OK debit and one-purchase limit. `scenes/clock_polish_test.tscn` remains green.
- MP4 decoded without errors; video stream is H.264 High, 1920 × 1080, 30 fps, and audio stream is stereo AAC at 48 kHz. Five encoded frames were reviewed at the hook, hit, altar, confirmation and closing title.
