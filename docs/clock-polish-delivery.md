# Clock battle polish delivery

16 September 2026. Native Godot implementation; existing Antigravity work preserved.

17 September update: the act-one Sentinel now has a separate real-time 3D encounter and a new portable build. See [the directed encounter delivery](directed-encounter-delivery.md). The notes below describe the earlier `build/polished` snapshot.

## Play

Run `build/polished/Overkill.exe`. Keep `Overkill.pck` beside it. This portable local build includes the official Godot 4.5.1 runtime and its MIT license. It does not replace the previous public installer.

## Implemented

- Persistent inventory of 18 physical clock relics: 12 assembled sockets and six reserves. Drafting, reserve swaps, reward selection, merchant purchases, removal, and tempering all use the same saved inventory. Removal retains at least 15 relics. Tempering previews the changes before committing and is limited to one upgrade per rest.
- Ten enemy profiles, with distinct damage/guard/status patterns, reverse rotation, opposing twin hands, multi-hit attacks, and currency siphoning. Enemy packs arrive as sequential reinforcements. Victory waits for the last enemy; defeat and victory fire once.
- New cathedral arena, forged clock faces, live sockets, energy paths, atmosphere, impact effects, camera feedback, staged choices, scene fades, and animated combatants.
- Eight character poses: idle, anticipation, strike, recovery, guard, hit, heavy anticipation, and defeat. Alpha-aware alignment preserves character scale across poses. Default presentation is illustrated 2.5D. An articulated procedural 3D experiment is available through `-- --3d-prototype`, but is not the default art direction.
- Original ambient music and mechanical sound effects with independent volume settings. Reduced camera motion setting.
- Updated collection, merchant, forge, reward, and shrine interactions. Fixed saved currency-history loading.

## Verification and limits

Integration checks cover persistent upgrades, save/load, legacy fallback, reserve floor, merchant affordability and duplicate purchases, ten enemy profiles, debuffs, reinforcements, reverse rotation, single defeat, and one rest upgrade. Battle smoke exercises 12 assembly hours, duplicate input rejection, four quadrant sweeps, actual reserve swaps, forward wrap, and one victory signal.

The portable executable was launched from its build directory without source-project arguments and initialized its rendered game using Intel OpenGL. The same packaged executable passed `CLOCK_SMOKE_OK` against the exported resources. Packaged startup and smoke logs contain no script errors or shutdown leak warnings.

Scripted encounters won the first-act fights with normal starting HP. Later boss fixtures use a fully upgraded inventory: act 2 boss finished with 12 HP, act 3 with 23 HP, and final boss with 1 HP. These are deterministic encounter checks, not exhaustive full-run balance certification. Rendered screenshots inspect all eight poses and the collection, merchant, and forge screens.

Old saves without clock inventory receive the 18-relic starter inventory; old card records are retained, but their upgrades are not converted into clock upgrades. Three character atlases cover the current enemy profiles; this is not a bespoke model and animation set for every enemy. Broader hardware performance testing and full-campaign playtesting remain production work. This delivery substantially improves the playable game; it is not a claim of complete AAA production quality.

The local environment reports a Windows root-certificate warning at engine startup. Headless shutdown also reports an audio playback resource leak despite explicit stream cleanup; no integration script errors were observed.
